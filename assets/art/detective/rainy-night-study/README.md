# Rainy Night Study

Standalone art assets for the detective game prototype.

`assets.json` is the machine-readable integration manifest for scenes and inventory clues.

- `cover.webp` — text-free case cover background, 1024 × 1536, opaque WebP; localized title must be overlaid in code.
- `study.webp` — main playable study background, 1024 × 1280, opaque WebP.
- `scene-02.webp` — mansion corridor and staircase background, 1024 × 1280, opaque WebP.
- `scene-03.webp` — rain-streaked window clue background, 1024 × 1280, opaque WebP.
- `desk-area.webp` — interactive writing-desk close-up, 1024 × 768, opaque WebP.
- `desk-drawer.webp` — open-drawer clue close-up, 1024 × 768, opaque WebP.
